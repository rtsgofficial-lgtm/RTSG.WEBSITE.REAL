Drayage Driver (“Drayage Driver,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when you use the Drayage Driver mobile application and related services.

Drayage Driver is designed as a local-first operational application for drayage drivers and small carriers. Many core features operate directly on your device without requiring an account or cloud connection.

By using Drayage Driver, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information You Provide

Depending on how you use Drayage Driver, you may provide information including:

Account Information

If you choose to create an account for cloud backup or synchronization, we may process:

  • Email address
  • Account identifier
  • Authentication information necessary to maintain your account and session

Authentication is provided through Supabase.

You are not required to create an account to use the core local features of Drayage Driver.

Load and Operational Information

Information you enter into the app may include:

  • Container numbers
  • Booking or bill-of-lading references
  • Pickup and delivery locations
  • Appointment dates and times
  • Last Free Day information
  • Chassis details
  • Steamship line information
  • Load status and operational milestones
  • Empty-return information
  • Notes associated with loads

Financial and Claim Information

You may choose to store information such as:

  • Load rates
  • Accessorial amounts
  • Detention amounts and rates
  • Expenses
  • Fuel, toll, parking, repair, chassis, scale, or other costs
  • Payment or claim statuses

Drayage Driver does not directly process credit-card payments for trucking services.

Photos, Documents, and Evidence

You may add photos or documents such as:

  • Proof of delivery
  • Equipment interchange receipts
  • Gate tickets
  • Container or chassis photos
  • Damage photos
  • Seal photos
  • Empty-return receipts
  • Scale tickets
  • Expense receipts
  • Other evidence related to a load

These files are stored locally on your device unless you choose to use cloud backup or synchronization.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Drayage Driver is designed to operate locally first.

Operational information is primarily stored in a local database on your device. This may include loads, detention records, accessorials, expenses, documents, claim information, settings, and synchronization metadata.

Local evidence files are stored in app-controlled storage on your device.

If you do not enable cloud features, this operational information is not uploaded to our cloud database merely because you use the app.

Deleting the app may cause locally stored information that has not been backed up to be lost.

3. Optional Cloud Backup and Synchronization

Drayage Pro may provide cloud backup, restore, and multi-device synchronization.

If you use these features, certain information may be transmitted to and stored using Supabase infrastructure, including:

  • Account identifiers
  • Load information
  • Status events
  • Detention records
  • Accessorial records
  • Expense records
  • Document metadata
  • Selected app settings
  • Evidence files you choose to back up

Cloud data is associated with your authenticated user account and is protected by access-control policies intended to prevent one user from accessing another user's records.

Cloud functionality is optional. Core local app functionality remains available without cloud synchronization.

4. Subscription and Purchase Information

Drayage Driver uses RevenueCat to manage subscription entitlements and communicate with the Apple App Store and other supported app stores.

RevenueCat may process information necessary to provide subscription functionality, including:

  • App user or customer identifiers
  • Device or operating-system information
  • Subscription status
  • Purchase and transaction information
  • Apple receipt information or equivalent store purchase tokens
  • Dates associated with purchases, renewals, or entitlement status

We do not receive or store your full credit-card number through RevenueCat.

Payments are processed by Apple or another applicable app-store provider under that provider's own terms and privacy practices.

RevenueCat acts as a service provider for subscription-management functionality and may process end-user transaction and technical information on our behalf.

5. Device Permissions

Depending on the features you use, Drayage Driver may request access to certain device capabilities.

Camera and Photo Library

Camera or photo-library access may be requested when you choose to capture or attach evidence.

Drayage Driver does not continuously access your camera or photo library.

Notifications

Notification permission may be requested if you enable operational reminders.

Notifications may be used for matters such as:

  • Pickup or delivery appointments
  • Last Free Day reminders
  • Operational deadlines
  • Other load-related reminders you configure

Notification permission is optional and may be disabled through your device settings.

6. Location Information

Drayage Driver does not currently use continuous GPS tracking, geofencing, or background location monitoring.

The app may allow you to open a location or address in your device's mapping application, but Drayage Driver does not currently maintain permanent driver-location tracking as part of its core service.

7. Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

Drayage Driver does not currently include third-party advertising networks.

We do not sell your personal information to advertisers.

We do not currently use your operational load, financial, claim, or evidence information for targeted advertising.

RevenueCat and other service providers may process limited technical information required to provide their services.

If our analytics or advertising practices materially change in the future, this Privacy Policy and applicable App Store privacy disclosures will be updated.

8. How We Use Information

We may use information processed through Drayage Driver to:

  • Provide app functionality
  • Store and organize your loads
  • Calculate detention and financial information
  • Generate claim-supporting information and proof packets
  • Back up and synchronize data when requested
  • Authenticate your account
  • Provide subscription features
  • Restore purchases
  • Maintain security and prevent unauthorized access
  • Diagnose technical problems
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not use your load information to dispatch freight, sell loads, or operate a freight marketplace.

9. How Information May Be Shared

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers necessary to operate Drayage Driver, including:

Supabase

Supabase provides optional authentication, database, and file-storage infrastructure used for cloud backup and synchronization.

RevenueCat

RevenueCat provides subscription-management and entitlement services and processes certain transaction and technical information necessary to manage purchases.

Apple

Apple processes purchases made through the App Store and may process information according to Apple's own privacy policies and terms.

We may also disclose information where required by law, legal process, or a valid governmental request, or where reasonably necessary to protect the rights, security, or integrity of Drayage Driver, its users, or others.

10. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information processed through Drayage Driver.

These safeguards include measures such as:

  • Local application storage
  • Authenticated cloud access
  • User-specific cloud-data access controls
  • Private cloud evidence storage
  • Encrypted network connections provided by our infrastructure providers

However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device and account credentials.

11. Data Retention

Locally stored information remains on your device until you delete it, remove the application, or otherwise clear the application's data.

Cloud-backed information may remain stored while your account or cloud backup remains active and for a period reasonably necessary to provide backup, synchronization, security, dispute-resolution, and legal-compliance functions.

Subscription transaction information may be retained by Apple, RevenueCat, or other applicable service providers according to their own retention requirements.

We may retain certain information where required by law or where necessary to resolve disputes, prevent abuse, or enforce applicable agreements.

12. Data Deletion and Account Requests

You may delete individual operational records through the app where deletion functionality is available.

If you use a Drayage Driver cloud account, you may request deletion of your cloud account and associated personal information by contacting us at:

rtsgmain@rtsg.org

We may need to verify your identity before processing certain requests.

Deleting a subscription through Apple does not automatically delete your Drayage Driver account or operational records.

Likewise, deleting your Drayage Driver account does not automatically cancel an App Store subscription. Subscriptions must be managed through your Apple account or applicable app store.

13. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including the right to:

  • Request access to personal information
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of certain information
  • Request restriction of certain processing
  • Request a copy of certain information
  • Object to certain processing
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

These rights may be subject to legal limitations and exceptions.

To make a privacy request, contact:

rtsgmain@rtsg.org

14. International Processing

Our service providers may process or store information in countries different from the country in which you live.

For example, third-party infrastructure providers may process information in the United States or other jurisdictions.

Those jurisdictions may have data-protection laws different from those of your home country.

15. Children's Privacy

Drayage Driver is intended for commercial transportation professionals and is not designed for children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13, or any higher minimum age required by applicable local law.

If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us.

16. Third-Party Services

Drayage Driver may interact with third-party services such as:

  • Supabase
  • RevenueCat
  • Apple App Store
  • Native mapping applications

Those third parties operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of services you use.

17. Proof Packets and User-Directed Sharing

Drayage Driver may allow you to generate and share Proof Packet PDFs or other documents.

These exports may contain information from loads and evidence that you choose to include.

When you use the device's sharing interface to send a document to another person or service, that transmission is initiated by you and becomes subject to the privacy practices of the receiving person, application, or service.

You are responsible for confirming that you are authorized to share the information contained in a Proof Packet or other exported document.

18. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in:

  • App functionality
  • Service providers
  • Legal requirements
  • Privacy practices

When we make material changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date above and may provide additional notice where appropriate.

Your continued use of Drayage Driver after an updated Privacy Policy becomes effective constitutes acknowledgment of the updated policy where permitted by law.
